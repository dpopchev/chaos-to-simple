Incident I3: cards charged for parcels we did not have
Support summary, week of 2026-10-23, shop orders

What happened: customers were charged in full for orders the warehouse could
not ship. The storefront said the item was in stock, the card was charged,
and the parcel was never packed.
Cause as far as we know: "out of stock" was written to a log line that
nobody read. No alert, no dashboard, no one on the floor was told.
Impact: 37 support tickets opened this week, all for the same pattern.
A refund took about a week to reach the customer's card, so most people
wrote in twice before they got their money back.
Next step: the order must be refused before the card is charged.
