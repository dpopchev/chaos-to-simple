## Hypothesis
Does placing the actor's request under the title and the tests and checkout diffs side by side under their own headings, Python-highlighted with only the scope and the changed rows, let pages 6 to 13 show both at scriptsize or larger?

## Outcome
Given the deck, When make talk, Then pages 6 and 8 to 13 each show the actor's request under the title, then the tests and checkout headings over their diffs (page 6 the whole v0 checkout, pages 8 to 13 side by side), page 7 the request beside the v0 tests, Python-highlighted, only the scope and the changed rows coloured, at scriptsize or larger with make widths and make overflow green

## Findings
- 2026-11-03 fact: page 6 (whole v0, 24 diff lines) stacked at scriptsize runs 30.5pt over the frame, 33.5pt short of 3pt slack; pages 7 to 12 fit -> one frame cannot hold v0 and its tests at scriptsize
- 2026-11-03 fact: story.lua (383e26d) writes Python rows with pandoc.write and no highlight_style (rejected by pandoc 3.11); the \KeywordTok macros and Verbatim come from the deck's own highlighted blocks -> a deck with no other highlighted block would not compile its story blocks

## Decisions
- 2026-11-03: layout: request block under the title (one shade), tests and checkout headings side by side (second shade) over their diffs (third shade); diff style always, scope row and changed rows only, Python-highlighted; page 6 split, pages 6 to 13; a row over 60 chars wrapped (developer)
- 2026-11-03: page 7: the request beside the v0 tests (30/70) accepted over the request under the title; story rows keep their colorbox padding, no row-spacing fix (developer)
