## Rule 1: how ifs count {paths=64}

- Sequential ifs multiply: 2 x 2 x 2 x 2 x 2 x 2
- `elif` and `match` alternatives add: 2 + 2 + 2
- A guard clause adds one
- Rewriting the two ifs of section 01 as if/elif: 4 paths become 3
- Compound conditions, `max()`, one-line conditionals: bits coverage cannot see

::: notes
The rest of the talk is a list of ways to turn a multiplication into an addition.
:::
