## A path is a six-bit number {paths=64}

```{.text include=build/01-bits/grid.txt}
```

- Sequential ifs multiply: 2 x 2 x 2 x 2 x 2 x 2
- `elif` and `match` alternatives add: 2 + 2 + 2
- Two sequential ifs as if/elif: 4 paths become 3

::: notes
Two of these sixty-four are covered: 000000 and 111111, the two tests. Point at them.
000001 is incident I1.
A guard clause adds one. Compound conditions, `max()`, one-line conditionals: bits
coverage cannot see.
The rest of the talk is a list of ways to turn a multiplication into an addition.
:::
